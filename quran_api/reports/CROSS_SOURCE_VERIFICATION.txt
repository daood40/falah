  a) ORTHOGRAPHY (Tanzil simple, Imlaei)
     Expected. The same words are written differently: ٱلصَّلَوٰة vs الصلاة,
     a hamza written as a letter vs marked on a carrier, and ya spellings.
     The decisive comparison is against an edition in OUR orthography
     (Hafs Uthmani), and that one agrees for every single ayah.

  b) JUZ 11 START — ours 9:93, quran-db 9:92
     The customary start of juz 11 is At-Tawbah 9:93
     (إِنَّمَا ٱلسَّبِيلُ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ يَسْتَـْٔذِنُونَكَ), which is what we store,
     following quran-meta (Hafs/Tanzil). The reference is one ayah early.
     All other 29 juz boundaries agree exactly. No change made.

  c) SAJDAH POSITIONS — 15 (ours) vs 14 (quran-db), 4 of them off by 1–2
     Two documented conventions: our source marks the ayah at whose END the
     prostration falls (16:50, 17:109, 27:26, 41:38) and includes the second
     sajdah of Al-Hajj (22:77); the reference marks the earlier ayah and
     lists 14. Both are legitimate; `sajdah_type` stays NULL because neither
     source states the ruling. An owner/scholar decision, not a bug.

  d) PAGE OF AN AYAH — 56 of 6,236 differ from the QCF v4 layout (0.90%)
     Our page numbers follow the Tanzil/quran-meta Madani list, and the
     604 page boundaries agree with quran-db exactly (604/604). The QCF v4
     typesetting places 56 ayahs on the adjacent page. Page numbering is a
     property of a printed edition, so this needs an owner decision about
     WHICH printed mushaf the app must match — it is not a data error.
